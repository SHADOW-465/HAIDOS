Recommendation: Ink & Iris for the balance of technical credibility and independent thinking across manufacturing and broader workflow work. Graphite & Oxide is the strongest alternative for a more overtly industrial identity. Pine & Mint is calmer and more approachable.

Proposed Ink & Iris website use: warm off-white for most surfaces; ink for text, logo, navigation and primary buttons; iris for restrained highlights and supporting panels. Use ink text on iris, not white body text on iris. Start around 75% neutral, 20% ink, 5% iris as visual guidance, not a rigid rule. Verify actual interface color combinations when implementing.

Selection should precede final logo refinement and vector production. Refine duplicate H treatments, spacing, negative-space details and small-size clarity. The small samples on these boards are illustrative; 16px and print reproduction are not yet validated. No trademark clearance was performed.

Positioning constraint: the user confirmed Company Brain has not been implemented. It is a future offering and must not be marketed as an existing deployed product.

Generation: built-in image tool, followed by edits to remove dark lighting and improve contrast. Exact initial and final-edit prompts are in prompts.md.
